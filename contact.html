A few things to know

How do I place an order?

Use a WhatsApp enquiry link on the site. The team can confirm the exact product, current price, availability, delivery options and payment arrangements directly with you.

Are prices shown online?

Prices are shown on the Products page where available. Please contact us to confirm current stock, final pricing and delivery before ordering.

Can I ask about my farm setup?

Yes. Share your goals and basic requirements. For bird health, disease, medication or vaccination decisions, consult a qualified poultry veterinarian.

Where do you deliver?

Delivery areas and costs must be confirmed with Eco Kuku before ordering.
