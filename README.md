# Eco Kuku Website

A custom WordPress theme starter for Eco Kuku, designed around a distinctive Kenyan poultry brand.

## Direction
- Fresh, professional forest-green and warm-cream visual identity
- Mobile-first layout and accessible navigation
- WooCommerce product catalogue
- WhatsApp-first product enquiries and ordering (no online payment gateway in this phase)
- Pages for Home, Shop, About, Poultry Farming Guides, and Contact

## Build status
Initial project setup. Theme files and setup instructions are being added next.

## Important
Use real Eco Kuku farm and product photography, verified product descriptions and prices, and the company's approved contact details. Do not publish placeholder contact details or unverified claims.
