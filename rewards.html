Referral rewards – terms

1. The “up to 200%” figure is the maximum reward rate available under the programme, subject to the qualifying activity and eligibility requirements.

2. Rewards, where applicable, are subject to review by the Eco Kuku team via WhatsApp. Registration or activation fees alone do not guarantee a reward.

3. A referral must be genuine and meet the programme’s eligibility and verification requirements. Self-referrals, duplicate accounts, misleading promotion and fraudulent activity are not eligible.

4. The qualifying products or services, reward calculation, confirmation process and any payment schedule must be communicated by Eco Kuku before a reward is earned. No reward is due solely because someone registers.

5. Reward reviews are handled weekly according to your registration date.

6. Eco Kuku may review or reject activity that does not meet these terms. Any changes to the programme will be communicated to members.
